Privacy

This notice covers rblch.com. The site has no analytics, advertising trackers, comments, contact forms, or accounts. Its code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Images are served with the site, and fonts come from your device.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. I do not receive these logs. Website delivery and security rely on my legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

GitHub may process this data in the United States, relying on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See GitHub’s Pages documentation and privacy statement for its processing, retention, and international transfers.

The feed

The RSS feed is a file served with the site. A feed reader that fetches it sends the same request details to GitHub as a visit to a page.

External links

Posts link to other sites, such as model pages and papers. Opening a link passes it to that site, whose privacy settings and policies then apply.

Contact

If you email support@rblch.com, I receive your address, message, and any attachments. I use them to respond and follow up. Please share only what is needed to explain your enquiry.

This processing relies on my legitimate interest in answering enquiries under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. I keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any related obligations or legal claims. You can request deletion by email.

Publisher and your rights

The publisher responsible for this site and their postal details are listed in the Impressum. For privacy questions or requests, email support@rblch.com.

Depending on the circumstances, applicable law gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data, and to receive a portable copy. You can also complain to a data-protection authority.
